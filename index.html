Duo-Plan Student Planner App

The Duo-Plan student planner app was designed to help students stay organized with assignments, schedules, and personal goals. I focused on creating a clean and simple design that would be easy for students to understand and use daily. During this project, I learned more about UI and UX design and how layout choices affect the user experience. I also improved my creativity and planning skills while designing the app screens and features. This project helped me better understand how technology can solve everyday problems for students.

Skills: UI Design, UX Design, Figma, Creativity, Organization
